Mystery Full Breakdown

The Killer: Mr Vampire

The Weapon: A Heavy Pumpkin

The Full Story:
At 3:50 p.m., Mrs Vampire gave her phone to Mr Vampire. At 3:55 p.m., Mr Vampire read her WhatsApp messages and realized his wife Wendy (Mrs Vampire) was secretly in love with Skeleton!

At 4:00 p.m., Skeleton went to the living room while Mrs Vampire went to the garden. Mr Vampire followed Skeleton into the living room, grabbed the heavy pumpkin by the window, dropped his candy, left a black hair on the window frame, smashed Skeleton's phone, and killed him at 4:05 p.m.!

He then escaped through the garden and came back through the front door at 4:10 p.m. pretending to make tea!
